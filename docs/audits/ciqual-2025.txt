CIQUAL CATALOGUE REPAIR — 2026-10-05
Source: Anses. 2025. Table de composition nutritionnelle des aliments Ciqual.
Official release: https://doi.org/10.57745/RDMHWY (version 1)
Licence: Etalab/Open Licence 2.0, https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/
ANSES XML release: 2025-11-03. Download API identifiers: 666252 (foods),
666249 (composition), 666246 (nutrients). Metadata:
https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.57745/RDMHWY

Four-nutrient adapted subset: EU energy 328, Jones protein 25000,
carbohydrate 31000, fat 40000. Names retain original MoovX punctuation.
3,484 unique local names match the official food names after accent and
punctuation normalization. Migration matches exact existing names, not IDs.
15 unmatched rows move to source=unverified, preserving IDs and values.

Raw strings are retained. Numeric values retain official precision; absent
values become NULL; traces use zero as an approximation; less-than values
use the stated upper bound as a conservative estimate, never literal zero.
The in-app disclosure explains these adaptations and links to source/licence.
The selectable_food_items security-invoker view excludes unverified sources
and incomplete energy/macronutrient records (161 Ciqual foods). Incomplete
reference records remain available for audit in food_items. Historic logs,
meal snapshots and saved meals are not rewritten. Fitness appears first.
